How the estimate works

Emissions (kg CO2e) = activity data × emission factor. Units are converted first, full precision is kept internally, and rounding happens only on screen.

  • Private vehicle: distance × trips × factor per vehicle-km ÷ occupants (a round trip counts twice).
  • Jeepney, bus: distance × trips × factor per passenger-km.
  • Food: amount in kg × factor per kg (a serving uses a stated serving size and is marked as estimated).
  • Waste: mass in kg × factor for that waste type and disposal method (a bag count uses a stated kg-per-bag assumption and is marked as estimated).
  • Electricity: kWh × grid factor ÷ household size. Cooking gas: kg × factor.
  • Water: litres × supply factor, plus heating energy when hot water is used. Flights: distance × factor per passenger-km. Hotels: nights × factor per room-night ÷ people sharing the room. Shopping and other activities are not calculated unless you enter an emission value with its source. Entries that cannot be calculated are saved as “Not calculated” and left out of totals.
  • Potential savings = baseline estimated emissions − alternative estimated emissions.

Emission factors used in this prototype

Factors with a named source are marked "sourced, not yet verified". Factors marked "demo, unverified" are still placeholders. Several sourced values come from the UK or from global averages, not from the Philippines, as stated in the Source column.

Limits of this prototype

  • Sign-in is a prototype (no password). Accounts, entries, and streaks are saved only in this browser. There is no consent form or server.
  • Entries can be added, edited, and deleted. Entries saved before editing was added must be re-entered to edit them.
  • No emission factor here has been independently verified. Some have a cited source (shown in the table above); the rest are demonstration placeholders. Several cited values are UK or global figures, not Philippine measurements.
  • Distance is typed in; route lookup and GPS are not included.
  • Recommendation rules cover private-vehicle trips, beef and pork meals, and disposal of biodegradable and recyclable waste. Thresholds are adjustable and are not scientific values.
  • The full system adds secure login, an admin page for verified factors, a pseudonymous research export, and automated tests of every formula.
